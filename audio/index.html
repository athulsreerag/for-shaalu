Archive II

Our first kiss

There's a version of me that existed only up until that day — unsure, a little too careful, still learning what it meant to actually love someone. Then you kissed me, and I understood something I hadn't before. First kiss. First touch. The first room I ever shared with someone the way I shared it with you.

Archive III

Kottayam, and losing you for a while

There was a stretch of time where you drifted somewhere I couldn't quite follow. Then came that train — Thalassery to Kottayam — and for a few hours everything felt the way it used to. We were close again, like no distance had ever come between us. But somewhere after that, without me even realizing it, you slipped far again. And Kottayam gave us the fight that broke my heart into a million pieces. I didn't know, for a while, if we'd find our way back to each other. But when you came to Bangalore, we did — one small walk at a time, until those small, ordinary moments quietly turned into a million memories of their own.

I have to thank your sister first — for introducing you into my life without even knowing what she was starting. And I have to thank the movies we both loved, the ones that somehow gave us a language before we had our own.

The first wallpaper I ever kept on my watch was a selfie of you in a Kerala saree. It's still my all-time favourite photo of you, and it hasn't lost that place since.

I do have one small regret — that even with all this time together, we still don't have enough good photos of just us. Mostly selfies, half-blurred, taken in a hurry. I want that to change.

I don't want a perfect love story. I want ours — with its beautiful moments, its difficult days, the laughter, the mistakes, and everything in between.

Every ordinary day

Our future, together

This becomes ours to keep adding to — photos with both our families, new countries, new food, new ordinary days worth remembering.

I know I can't just ask you to wait for me. But I am asking you to choose me — to keep choosing me, even while I'm still becoming who I need to be.

I promise you this: I will use every opportunity that comes my way, and work harder than I ever have, to reach a place where I'm truly ready — where I have the standing to actually marry you, not just hope to.

There will be hurdles. I know that already. But if we keep trying, if we keep choosing each other, I believe it will happen. I'll give it everything I have, and I trust that God will carry the rest.

I won't pretend I'm not scared, or that I know whether this road ahead will be easy. But whatever it takes, I want you to choose me. And I promise you — I will spend everything I have making sure you're happier with me than you could ever be with anyone else in this world. Whatever you've wanted for your life, I want to be the one who helps you get there.

Yours, again and again.
